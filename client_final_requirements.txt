  SECTION A — BUG FIXES (HIGH PRIORITY)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

[BUG-01] SHARING & COPYING BUTTONS DO NOT WORK
  - The Share button and Copy button are broken.
  - They must be fixed and work correctly.

[BUG-02] PRAYER TIMES ARE INCORRECT (VERY IMPORTANT)
  - Prayer times can be off by up to ~1 hour compared to
    the Islamic Center's official times.
  - Fix: Ensure correct location, calculation method,
    time zone, daylight saving time, and prayer time
    adjustments are all applied properly.
  - Also: User could not find Hungary location manually.
    -> Add auto GPS detection for location.
    -> Or fix the manual location search to include Hungary.

[BUG-03] QIBLA DIRECTION IS SOMETIMES INCORRECT (IMPORTANT)
  - The Qibla compass is not always accurate.
  - Sometimes correct, sometimes wrong direction.
  - Fix: Check compass calibration, GPS location data,
    and Qibla calculation algorithm.
  - Ensure direction is stable and accurate at all times.

[BUG-04] HADITHS AFTER SAHIH AL-BUKHARI NOT LOADING
  - All Hadith collections after Sahih al-Bukhari either
    do not load or are missing entirely.
  - Fix: Check all Hadith collections and ensure every
    available Hadith is properly loaded and accessible.

[BUG-05] TAFSIR OFTEN DOES NOT LOAD (IMPORTANT)
  - Tafsir frequently fails to load - shows "unavailable"
    or "not available" message.
  - Fix: Check all Tafsir sources and fix loading issue.
  - Tafsir must load reliably whenever it is available.
  - When user taps on an Arabic verse, the Tafsir/explanation
    should appear in the user's selected language.

[BUG-06] TAFSIR LANGUAGE ISSUES — HUNGARIAN & GERMAN
  - When Hungarian is selected: Tafsir is displayed in a
    wrong/different language instead of Hungarian.
  - When Hungarian or German is selected: Tafsir content
    is missing for individual Ayahs.
  - Fix: Tafsir must match the app's selected language.

[BUG-07] ARABIC TEXT MISSING IN HUNGARIAN MODE
  - When Hungarian is selected as the app language,
    the Quran reading screen shows ONLY the Hungarian
    translation — the original Arabic text is missing.
  - Fix: Always display the Arabic text TOGETHER with
    the selected translation (e.g. Hungarian, German, etc.)

[BUG-08] SURAH AL-FATIHA — WRONG LANGUAGE (HUNGARIAN)
  - When Hungarian is selected, Surah Al-Fatiha is shown
    in English instead of Hungarian.
  - All other Surahs show correctly in Hungarian.
  - Fix: Apply Hungarian translation to Surah Al-Fatiha.

[BUG-09] QURAN RECITER SELECTION DOES NOT APPLY IMMEDIATELY
  - After selecting a new reciter, user must:
      1. Select the reciter
      2. Leave the Quran section
      3. Return to the Quran section
      4. Only then does the reciter work
  - Fix: The newly selected reciter must apply IMMEDIATELY
    without any need to leave and return to the section.

[BUG-10] QURAN AUDIO PLAYBACK — AYAH NUMBER NOT UPDATING
  - When user presses Next button (e.g. from Ayah 32),
    the Ayah number on screen does not update to 33.
  - Audio and displayed Ayah number are out of sync.
  - Fix: Sync the displayed Ayah number with audio playback.

[BUG-11] AUDIO PLAYER DOES NOT CLOSE WITH ONE TAP
  - When closing the audio player/window, user has to
    close/tap multiple times to stop the audio.
  - Fix: One close action must fully close the player
    and stop/remove the audio.

[BUG-12] GREEN READING BAR CAUSES AYAHS TO JUMP
  - While reading the Quran, the green reading progress bar
    jumps left and right, causing Ayahs to move around.
  - Fix: The green bar should ONLY move straight DOWN
    vertically. It must NOT cause any horizontal movement
    or make Ayahs jump around.

[BUG-13] HASANAT COUNTER — INCONSISTENT NUMBERS (IMPORTANT)
  - The Hasanat counter shows different numbers each time
    for the same Surah.
  - Example: Read Surah Al-Falaq, collect Hasanat, read
    again — a different number appears.
  - Fix:
      * The Hasanat calculation must be technically correct.
      * Numbers must NOT randomly change after collecting.
      * Check ALL Surahs individually.
      * Counting/reward logic must be consistent.
  - Note: Slightly lower than expected is acceptable,
    but must be stable and never random.

[BUG-14] BOOKS — APP FREEZES / HANGS WHEN READING
  - App often freezes or hangs when opening/reading a book
    in the Books category, even on a strong connection.
  - Fix: Investigate and fix the loading/performance issue.

[BUG-15] BOOKS & FATWAS — SCROLLING CAUSES ERROR/CRASH
  - Scrolling within Books and Fatwas library categories
    causes an error or crash.
  - Fix: Investigate and fix the scrolling crash bug.

[BUG-16] GERMAN LANGUAGE — INCORRECT CONTENT IN BOOKS
  - When app language is German, the Books category shows
    strange/incorrect text that does not make sense.
  - Fix: Review and correct German content/translations
    in the Books category.

[BUG-17] SEARCH CATEGORIES NOT LOADING CONTENT
  - In the Search section (2nd tab from left), these
    categories load NO content:
      * Guidance
      * Forgiveness
      * History
      * Patience
  - These categories DO work correctly:
      * Charity
      * Faith
  - Fix: Make all search category topics load correctly.

[BUG-18] ADHAN SOUND BUTTON IN SETTINGS DOES NOT WORK
  - The bell/Adhan button in Settings (top-right corner)
    does nothing when pressed.
  - Fix: When pressed, show a popup with available Adhan
    sounds where user can:
      * View all available Adhan sounds
      * Select an Adhan sound
      * Preview / Play the Adhan sound

[BUG-19] PREVIOUS/NEXT ADHAN SOUND BUTTONS DON'T WORK
  - In the Adhan Sound section, the two white navigation
    buttons next to the Play button do not work.
  - Fix:
      * Previous button -> play the previous Adhan sound
      * Next button -> play the next Adhan sound
  - Users must be able to switch between sounds easily.

[BUG-20] ARABIC FONT OVERLAPS BOOKMARK ICON
  - Arabic script slightly overlaps into the bookmark
    button/icon area.
  - Fix: Adjust font/layout so Arabic text does not
    overlap with the bookmarks icon. (Minor issue)
